Jace account

Sign in with your Jace account to sync bookmarks, passwords, history, open tabs, settings, and the sites you're signed in to between all your Jace Browsers.

Sync

Your data is encrypted on this device with a key made from this passphrase before it goes to Jace, so Jace can't read it. Jace can't recover it either: if you forget it, you can only start sync over.

What to sync

Site sign-ins syncs cookies, so when you sign in to Jace on another computer you're also signed in to the sites you use. Some sites tie a sign-in to one device and will still ask you to sign in.

Profiles

Each profile has its own tabs, history, passwords, add-ons and Jace account. Open one and it gets its own window.

WebUSB

A site only gets a device after you pick it. Keyboards, mice, USB sticks, webcams, and other protected kinds of interface can't be used with WebUSB.

Sites with USB access

None.

DRM (protected video)

Jace Browser uses Google's Widevine, which it downloads the first time a site needs it, on Windows, macOS, and Linux. Some services limit browsers they don't recognize to lower resolutions.

Privacy

Mozilla's data collection is switched off and locked, so add-ons, studies and remote experiments can't turn it back on:

  • Telemetry, Glean and usage reports
  • Studies and remote experiments (Normandy, Nimbus)
  • Crash reports
  • Sponsored tiles, stories and suggestions, and their click tracking
  • Ad measurement in web pages ("privacy-preserving attribution")
  • Personalized add-on and feature recommendations
  • Background pings: connectivity and captive portal checks, region lookup
  • Mozilla accounts and Firefox's own updater (Jace accounts and Jace updates replace them)

Kept on, because turning them off would make you less safe or break something:

  • Safe Browsing: warns about malware and phishing sites, using Google's lists checked by partial hash.
  • Security lists from Mozilla's Remote Settings: revoked certificates and dangerous add-ons.
  • Widevine download for DRM video, and add-on updates from addons.mozilla.org.
  • Hotel and airport Wi-Fi sign-in pages no longer pop up by themselves (that check pinged Mozilla). Open any http:// page to get to them.
