Jace account

Sign in with your Jace account to sync with Jace Browser and your other Firefoxes, and to stay signed in to Jace websites.

Sync

Your data is encrypted in Firefox with a key made from this passphrase before it goes to Jace, so Jace can't read it. Jace can't recover it either: if you forget it, you can only start sync over.

What to sync

Site sign-ins syncs cookies, so you're signed in to the same sites as on your other devices. Some sites tie a sign-in to one device and will still ask. Passwords can't be synced by an extension; Jace Browser syncs them.
