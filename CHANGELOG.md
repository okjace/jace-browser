# Changelog

## 1.0.1
The first release of **Jace Browser**, built on **Firefox 159.0**.

- **Window buttons match your system**: GNOME-style on Linux, Windows' own on Windows, traffic lights on macOS (Jace and Minimal styles still available).
- **No Mozilla terms-of-use screen** on first start.
- **Signed in to Jace websites automatically**: signing in to Jace Browser signs you in to the Jace website, and Jace's own apps skip the sign-in screen. Turn it off in about:jace.
- **Vertical tab styles**: names and icons, only icons, or only icons that grow when you hover.
- **Profiles button** to switch between profiles (each with its own tabs, passwords and Jace account). Hide it any time.
- **Jace-styled** tab close buttons, menus and panels, in your accent color.
- **Startup animation**: the Jace logo draws itself in and zooms away to the browser. Press any key to skip, or turn it off in about:jace.
- **First-run setup**: choose your tabs, accent color and look, import from another browser, sign in with Jace and turn on sync, and make Jace Browser your default.
- **Mozilla's tracking removed**: telemetry, studies, crash reports, sponsored content, ad measurement, recommendations and background pings are off and locked. See about:jace → Privacy.
- **Automatic updates**: Jace Browser moves to each new Firefox release by itself, and installed copies download, verify and install new versions on their own.
- **Jace Browser**, a Firefox-based browser with a Zen-style look: one soft surface, pages floating as rounded cards, pill tabs, and Jace green.
- **Vertical tabs, your choice.** On by default; turn them off (or back on) with the tabs button in the toolbar, **Ctrl+Alt+V** (**Cmd+Opt+V** on macOS), or about:jace. Compact mode hides the sidebar until you hover the window edge.
- **Jace account.** Sign in with Jace from the account button. Your name and picture show in the toolbar.
- **Jace sync**, end-to-end encrypted with a sync passphrase only you know: bookmarks, history, passwords, open tabs, settings, your add-on list, and **site sign-ins**. Sign in to Jace on a new computer, enter your sync passphrase once, and you're signed in to the sites you use on your other devices.
- **WebUSB** (`navigator.usb`), which Firefox doesn't have. Sites ask first, you pick the device, and keyboards, USB sticks, webcams and other protected devices are off-limits. Manage access in about:jace.
- **DRM**: Widevine is on for Windows, macOS and Linux, so Netflix, Disney+, Spotify and other protected video and music play.
- Installers for Windows (setup .exe and portable .zip), macOS (.dmg for Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux (AppImage and .tar.xz), built and tested by GitHub Actions.
